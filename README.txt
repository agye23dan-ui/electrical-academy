Electrical Academy (PWA) - NVTI General Electrical Certificate One & Two study companion, with international standards, solar PV and General Paper modules.
Deploy: upload every file in this folder together to an HTTPS host, open index.html, then Add to Home screen.
Files: index.html (app), elec1.js to elec10.js (content; elec7-elec10 are the depth expansions), mathfix.js (turns $...$ maths into readable text), bench.js (the extra simulators on the Bench page), houseengine.js, housegen.js and houseui.js (House Wiring Practice at #/house: choose rooms, place accessories, wire and terminate, then switch on; a circuit solver shows faults and RCD and MCB trips), cg0.js to cg5.js (City & Guilds 2365 Level 2 lessons and question banks), cl0.js to cl5.js (Level 3), elec12.js and elec13.js (realistic test drawings), cgviews.js (the City & Guilds hub, lesson banner and unit tests), manifest.json, icon.svg, sw.js (offline).
When you change a file, change the cache name in sw.js (currently ela-v27).
In content files, write backslashes in maths as double (\\frac) so JavaScript keeps them.
Topic coverage was checked against NCCER Electrical Level 1-2 and City & Guilds 2365 headings; the official NVTI syllabus is not published online, so check it against NVTI's own syllabus.

elec14.js and elec15.js hold the explanations for every question (matched by question text; they replace the short 'Answer: ...' notes). elec16.js adds twelve extra schematics for City & Guilds modules.
The cache is refreshed fresh on install, so changing the cache name in sw.js is all that is needed.
This course is aligned to NVTI, City & Guilds 2365 and BS 7671 topics but is not accredited or approved by any of those bodies. UK rules (BS 7671, CDM, Part P) are taught for City & Guilds; check local Ghana rules for practice.
